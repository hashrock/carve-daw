The playlist and piano roll, with the Neon Streets sample song. Paint clips, edit a pattern, and every copy of it changes.

A mock for getting the feel of it. The sounds are simple stand-ins for the app's instruments.

Vocal Synthesis

Type lyrics on notes for UTAU voicebanks or the built-in modelled voice.

Playlist

Edit a pattern once, update every copy.

Piano Roll

Notes, velocity, quantize, swing.

Built-in Instruments

Synths, analog drums, and modelled instruments ready to play.

Mixer & Effects

Inserts, sends, sidechain, EQ, and Overtop 3-band compression.
