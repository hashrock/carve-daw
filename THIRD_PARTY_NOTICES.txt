from the About window, and it is copied into the application bundle at
Contents/Resources/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt.

Two distributions are built from the same source. The source and the GitHub
Releases disk images are under the GNU Affero General Public License, version
3 or later (see the LICENSE file and https://www.gnu.org/licenses/agpl-3.0.html).

that licence. It is built under the JUCE 8 Starter licence and the Tracktion
Engine Personal licence, and it is delivered under Apple's App Store terms.

Recordings behind Acoustic Drums and Electric Bass
--------------------------------------------------

The two instruments contain no samples. Each plays from an analysis of a
recording -- partials, envelopes and the first milliseconds of each hit or
note -- compiled into the application from assets/instruments. The
recordings analysed were released into the public domain, and are credited
here as thanks rather than as a licence condition:

    Acoustic Drums: Virtuosity Drums, CC0 1.0.
        https://github.com/sfzinstruments/virtuosity_drums
    Electric Bass: Growlybass by Karoryfer Samples, CC0 1.0.
        https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.growlybass
